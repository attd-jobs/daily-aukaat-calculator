Calculation logic: Entry is manual. Initial SL = Entry ± selected SL%. Qty = Risk / SL distance. Target = SL distance × R:R from Entry. Exit is manual. P&L = Entry-to-Exit × Qty. SL, Target, Qty and P&L are editable. If you edit one of these, it is respected until the inputs that drive it are changed. Actual R:R = actual reward ÷ actual risk.
